# Paint colors carry gameplay rules

Paint will be more than a cosmetic decal: Paint Colors will carry gameplay rules for traversal, gates, camouflage, and tongue interaction. The MVP will start with Green Paint for Grip Surfaces, Purple Paint for Camouflage, and Orange Paint for Tongue Anchor activation so the game has a strong identity without turning the first implementation into an unbounded systems sandbox.

## Considered Options

- Cosmetic paint only.
- Paint for camouflage only.
- Paint for gates only.
- Paint for grip or traversal only.
- Paint as a compact rule system combining camouflage, gates, traversal permissions, and tongue interaction.

## Consequences

The level design must teach colors as rules, not just visual feedback. Automated browser checks should exercise at least one paint-driven traversal rule, one paint-driven gate, one camouflage consequence, and one paint-enabled tongue interaction.

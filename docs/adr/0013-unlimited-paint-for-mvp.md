# Unlimited paint for MVP

Paint will be unlimited for the MVP rather than ammo-limited, recharge-based, pickup-fed, or pool-bound. Early implementation needs to prove Paint Rules and Raycast Aim are fun and reliable before adding resource pressure.

## Considered Options

- Unlimited Paint.
- Paint has ammo per color.
- Paint recharges over time.
- Paint comes from level pickups.
- Paint is only available at paint pools.

## Consequences

Paint economy should not obscure whether targeting, gates, Camouflage, Grip Surfaces, and Tongue Anchor activation work. Ammo or recharge constraints can be revisited after the core loop is browser-verified.

# First playable acceptance

The first MVP must prove movement, Orbit Camera control, collision, jumping, Play HUD feedback, and reaching an Exit before commit and push. A nonblank scene or movement-only prototype would not establish the body-camera-world contract needed for later paint, stealth, and tongue mechanics.

## Considered Options

- App boots and renders a nonblank scene.
- Nonblank scene plus Chameleon movement.
- Movement, camera orbit, collision, jump, HUD, and reaching an Exit.
- All mechanics across all levels.
- Visual polish only.

## Consequences

MVP 1 should include browser evidence for the core third-person path: load the game, move the Chameleon, orbit the camera, collide with the world, jump, read the Play HUD, and complete Training Grove by reaching the Exit.

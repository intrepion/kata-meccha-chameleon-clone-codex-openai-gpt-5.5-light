# Paint milestone acceptance

The paint milestone must prove switching between Paint Colors, painting a target, opening a Gate, creating a Grip Surface, and updating the Play HUD. Visual paint alone is not enough, because the game identity depends on Paint Rules changing traversal and progression.

## Considered Options

- Paint appears visually.
- Switch between Green Paint, Purple Paint, and Orange Paint.
- Paint a target, open a Gate, create a Grip Surface, and update the Play HUD.
- Complete all three Levels.
- Paint economy and collectibles.

## Consequences

MVP 2 should include browser evidence that Paint Rules affect gameplay, not only rendering. Paint economy remains out of scope for this milestone.

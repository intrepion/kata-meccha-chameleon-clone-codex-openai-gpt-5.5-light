# Handcrafted level progression

Meccha Chameleon will progress through three short handcrafted levels rather than a single sandbox, procedural run, hub, or score-only loop. Training Grove will teach movement and an Exit, Sentry Shrine will teach Purple Paint and Camouflage pressure, and Anchor Falls will teach Orange Paint, Tongue Anchors, and Green Paint traversal. This gives the project concrete acceptance evidence: each level can introduce and prove a distinct mechanic while keeping the browser clone small enough to finish with polish.

## Considered Options

- One polished sandbox arena.
- Three short levels with exits.
- A hub with challenge rooms.
- Endless generated rooms.
- Score attack.

## Consequences

The first implementation should be sliced around playable level progression: Training Grove as the first playable, Sentry Shrine as the stealth layer, and Anchor Falls as the tongue and grip finale.

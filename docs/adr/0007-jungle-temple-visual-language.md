# Jungle temple visual language

Meccha Chameleon will use a bright Jungle Temple visual language rather than an industrial lab, abstract test chamber, or swamp. Bright jungle color supports readable paint interactions, while temple structures provide vertical traversal landmarks and a stronger adventure identity.

## Considered Options

- Bright toy-like jungle obstacle course.
- Industrial lab full of paint machines.
- Ruined temple reclaimed by neon plants.
- Minimal abstract test chambers.
- Saturday-morning cartoon swamp.

## Consequences

Art direction must prioritize readable paint surfaces, recognizable traversal landmarks, and enough contrast for the Chameleon to stay visible against painted geometry.

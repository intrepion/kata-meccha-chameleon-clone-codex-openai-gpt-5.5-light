# MVP audio scope

The MVP will include movement, Paint, Tongue, Sentry alert, Sunfly, Exit, and Ambient Loop audio rather than no audio, UI-only sounds, a full adaptive soundtrack, or procedural music. Audio should support feel and feedback without competing with core mechanic implementation.

## Considered Options

- No audio.
- UI sounds only.
- Movement, Paint, Tongue, Sentry alert, Sunfly, Exit, and Ambient Loop.
- Full adaptive soundtrack.
- Procedural music.

## Consequences

Audio validation should confirm that major player feedback events can produce sound in the browser. Adaptive music and procedural scoring are not MVP requirements.

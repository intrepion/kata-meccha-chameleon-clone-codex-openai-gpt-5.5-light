# Final mechanics acceptance

The final mechanics milestone must prove Raycast Aim for Tongue Anchor activation, Sentry detection with Checkpoint reset, Sunfly collection, and completion of Training Grove, Sentry Shrine, and Anchor Falls. This is the minimum evidence that the clone's tongue, stealth, collectibles, and progression loops work together.

## Considered Options

- Tongue animation only.
- Sentry detection only.
- Raycast Tongue Anchor activation, Sentry Checkpoint reset, Sunfly collection, and completion of all three Levels.
- Visual polish only.
- Performance benchmark only.

## Consequences

MVP 3 should be verified through real browser play paths across all three Levels. Animation, polish, or isolated state checks cannot substitute for completing the progression loop.
